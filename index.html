About Us

Goshen Farms Snacks & Treats Limited (also known as Goshen Farms Quick Bites Co) is a leading FoodTech company based in Alimosho, Lagos. Founded by a Beverage Tech Strategist with a passion for quality and innovation, we specialize in delivering fresh, delicious snacks and quick bites that satisfy your cravings while meeting the highest standards of hygiene and taste.

Our mission is simple: to make nutritious, affordable snacking accessible to every Nigerian household. From our facility at 8 Omoyeni Street, AKESAN, Igando, we serve communities across Lagos with commitment, care, and consistency. At Goshen Farms, every bite tells a story of freshness and excellence.

Our Products & Services

We offer an extensive range of snacks and quick bites perfect for every occasion:

Premium Snack Packages

Carefully curated selections of Nigerian favorites including plantain chips, chin-chin, coconut candy, and groundnuts. Perfect for parties, offices, or personal enjoyment.

Quick Bites on Demand

Fresh puff-puff, meat pie, fish rolls, sausage rolls, and samosas prepared daily and delivered hot to your location.

Beverage Solutions

Complementary drink options including zobo, chapman, fresh fruit juices, and soft drinks to pair perfectly with our snacks.

Bulk & Corporate Orders

Special packages for events, conferences, staff meetings, and celebrations. We handle orders of any size with professionalism and punctuality.

Custom Snack Boxes

Personalized gift boxes and hampers for special occasions, branded packaging available for corporate gifts.

All our products are made with quality ingredients, packaged hygienically, and delivered fresh. We combine traditional Nigerian recipes with modern food safety standards.
