About Us

Nulis Educonsult and Allied Services, together with Hi-range Educonsult and Allied Ventures, represents a commitment to manufacturing excellence in the heart of Alimosho, Lagos. Our combined expertise spans diverse manufacturing processes, from precision machining to quality assembly and production management. Based at 8 Omoyeni Street, Akesan, Igando, we serve businesses across Lagos State and beyond with reliable, high-quality manufacturing solutions. Our team combines technical knowledge with practical experience to deliver products that meet international standards while understanding the unique requirements of the Nigerian market. Whether you need custom manufacturing, production consulting, or allied industrial services, we bring professionalism, efficiency, and integrity to every project.
